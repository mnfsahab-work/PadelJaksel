How the numbers are made

  • Court-hour booked = an AYO slot marked unavailable. Hours blocked by the venue itself (coaching, maintenance, members) look the same on AYO and are counted too.
  • Market = all padel venues AYO lists in Kota Jakarta Selatan. Padel simulators are excluded. Venues that take bookings outside AYO only show their AYO calendar.
  • Brands: chains are ranked as one brand. Air Padel = AIR Padel + AIR Padel Active. Republic Padel, Metropolar Padel and Padel Parc are grouped the same way.
  • Past days: AYO hides slots once they start, so a job saves every slot's last seen state every 30 minutes. Days before tracking began have no data; the first tracked day is partial.
  • Upcoming days show bookings made so far and will grow until the day is played.
