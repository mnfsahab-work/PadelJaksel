How the numbers are made

  • Court-hour booked = an AYO slot marked unavailable. Hours a venue blocks itself (coaching, maintenance, members) look the same on AYO and are counted too.
  • Market = padel venues on AYO inside the chosen area. "Within 5 / 10 km" is straight-line distance from any of the selected brand's venues; "Same city / province" uses the city and province AYO lists for each venue. Padel simulators are excluded. Bookings taken outside AYO are not visible.
  • Brands: the selected venue is evaluated together with its sister venues (e.g. AIR Padel + AIR Padel Active). Known chains are grouped when ranking by brand; switch to "Venue" to rank each location separately.
  • Past days: AYO hides slots once they start, so a job saves every slot's last seen state every 30 minutes. Days before tracking began have no data; the first tracked day is partial.
  • Upcoming days show bookings made so far and will grow until the day is played.
